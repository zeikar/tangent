# Korean wording

The channel's rules for Korean it puts out: scripts, descriptions, and
replies to viewers. They sit on top of the general rules in the global
`korean-polish` skill, `~/.claude/skills/korean-polish/rules.md` (kept in
the dotfiles repo): read those first; where the two differ, this file wins.
Spoken lines add the rules for the ear in `docs/narration.md`.

Add a channel-only rule here, dated, with the before and after; a rule
about Korean in general goes in the global file.

- **해요체 in scripts and descriptions,** one register with no -습니다: the
  narration's voice, and the description reads like the video. Replies to
  viewers may mix in 합니다 per the general rules.
- **Reply facts from research.md.** A reply about an episode says what its
  research.md supports. Anything else (a number computed on the spot, a
  fact from memory) is flagged to the human, who decides before posting.
